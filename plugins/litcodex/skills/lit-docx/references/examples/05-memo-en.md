# Move overview

The Finance and Procurement teams move from the third floor to the newly refitted fifth floor between Friday 14 and Monday 17 November. The third floor closes for its own refit on 18 November. The move covers 74 desks, two meeting rooms and the shared print area. Everyone keeps the same desk group and the same neighbours; only the floor and the room numbers change.

# Dates and individual tasks

On Thursday 13 November, crates arrive at each desk by 10:00. Pack personal items and paper files by 16:00 on Friday 14 November, label every crate with the orange label set left on your desk, and leave monitors and docking stations where they are; the moving team handles all IT equipment. Over the weekend the moving team carries the crates and sets up the new desks. On Monday 17 November, start the day on the fifth floor. Your crates will be at your new desk, and the IT desk will be on the fifth floor from 08:00 to 12:00 to fix any connection problems.

# Desks without a fixed place

Eight people who work mostly from home will use bookable desks on the fifth floor instead of fixed desks. They have already been contacted. Lockers for their equipment are next to the kitchen, and keys are issued at the reception desk from 17 November.

# Meeting rooms

The two third-floor rooms close on 13 November. Bookings for 14 to 21 November have been moved to rooms 5.04 and 5.06, and the booking system shows the new room for each meeting. Larger meetings that week can use the ground-floor training room, which is free every afternoon.

# Questions

Send questions to the Workplace Services inbox by Wednesday 12 November so they can be answered before packing starts. Anyone who needs help lifting crates or has equipment that needs special handling should say so in the same message.
