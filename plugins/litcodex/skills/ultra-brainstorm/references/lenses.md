# Lenses for idea agents

Idea agent `i` takes lens `i mod 32`. Each lens is one question an agent can act on without any
other context. Keep the list at 32 entries or more; change wording only to make a lens sharper.

1. Opposite goal: what does the reverse of the aim look like, and which piece of it is worth keeping?
2. One constraint gone: remove a real limit; what opens up, and what still works once it returns?
3. Borrowed mechanism: which distant field already solved a problem of this shape, and how?
4. The hardest user: build for the most rushed, most expert or most new person in the room.
5. Less: what can be taken away so the rest gets better?
6. Unlikely pair: join two things that are never used together.
7. Other decade: how would this be done twenty years ago, or twenty years ahead?
8. Plentiful substitute: replace the scarce input with one there is too much of.
9. Bad idea turned: write the worst answer, then flip its worst trait.
10. Better question: answer the question that should have been asked.
11. By tomorrow: what version costs almost nothing and works within a day?
12. Ten times: what would make this ten times better, whatever it costs?
13. Do nothing: what happens with no change, and when is that the right call?
14. Runs alone: what could happen with no person deciding each time?
15. By hand: what would someone do manually before any tool existed?
16. Default switch: which default, if flipped, changes what everyone does?
17. Safe failure: how does it break, and what design makes a break harmless?
18. Instant yes: what would make a careful, sceptical user agree at once?
19. Count it: what could be measured, and which idea does the measurement suggest?
20. Loop back: where can each result improve the next attempt?
21. Neighbours: who else touches this, and what would they build on top?
22. Smallest unit: what is the one-person, one-afternoon version?
23. Crowd scale: what changes at ten thousand users or items?
24. New medium: text, voice, image, sound, motion or a physical object.
25. New place: terminal, phone, paper, a meeting room, a car.
26. Who gains: who is rewarded today, and what if someone else were?
27. Reorder: swap the order of the steps; what improves?
28. All at once or a trickle: batch it, or stream it in small pieces?
29. Ritual from elsewhere: a checklist, ceremony or habit from another craft.
30. Teach it: what would make this easy to explain to a newcomer in a minute?
31. No experts: what if nobody involved knew the field at all?
32. Contrary bet: which belief everyone here shares is most likely wrong?
