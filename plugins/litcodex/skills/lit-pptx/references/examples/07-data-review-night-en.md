## Options for cast iron repair delays

:::: columns 1fr 1fr
::: col
- **Option A: framework road permits**
  (1) Effect: permit time falls from 2.3 to 0.5 days on arterial roads
  (2) Cost: €40,000 a year in fees, no new crews
  (3) Lead time: agreement with the roads department by January 2027
  (4) Risk: depends on the roads department accepting a yearly framework
:::
::: col
- **Option B: two more night crews**
  (1) Effect: repairs at night avoid permits on 60% of arterial jobs
  (2) Cost: €520,000 a year in staff and overtime
  (3) Lead time: recruiting and training until April 2027
  (4) Risk: night work raises noise complaints in residential streets
:::
::::

- Source: Example utility operations planning estimates, September 2026 (example)
