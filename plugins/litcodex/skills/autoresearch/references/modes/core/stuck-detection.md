# Stuck detection and pivots

## Codex-native contract

The picker loads this guidance; approved work runs through native `start-work` and `lit-loop`.
Pivot artifacts remain `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and cannot programmatically claim family completion.
Changed evaluator or budget requires re-planning and review.

Detect stagnation from repeated evaluator receipts. Record rejected hypothesis families and choose a
materially different variable still inside the approved proposal. A new dependency, root, action,
source, metric, evaluator, or budget pauses for re-planning. `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` is non-success.
