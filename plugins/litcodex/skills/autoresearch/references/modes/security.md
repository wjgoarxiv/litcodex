# Security review mode

## Codex-native contract

Select security in the picker; hand authorized probes/writes to native `start-work` and `lit-loop`.
Findings remain `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and cannot programmatically claim family completion. Changed
evaluator or budget requires re-planning and review.

Map assets, actors, trust boundaries, flows, entry points, and controls. Apply STRIDE and relevant
OWASP checks with safe evidence. No exploitation, credential use, denial of service, persistence,
external scanning, or secret collection occurs without specific authorization. Budget exhaustion
never means secure.
