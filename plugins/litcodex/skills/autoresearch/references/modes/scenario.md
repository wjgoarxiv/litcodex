# Scenario exploration mode

## Codex-native contract

Select scenario in the picker; hand approved probes/artifacts to native `start-work` and `lit-loop`.
Outputs remain `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and cannot programmatically claim family completion. Changed
evaluator or budget requires re-planning and review.

Define decision, horizon, invariants, levers, uncertainties, and evidence thresholds. Cover baseline,
favorable, adverse, edge, abuse, interruption, stale-state, dirty-tree, exhaustion, and recovery.
Record trigger, mechanism, impact, observables, mitigation, and residual risk.
