# STRIDE threat-model worksheet

## Codex-native contract

The picker loads this worksheet; approved checks use native `start-work` and `lit-loop`. Findings
remain `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and cannot programmatically claim family completion. Changed evaluator or
budget requires re-planning and review.

For each trust crossing assess spoofing, tampering, repudiation, disclosure, denial of service, and
privilege elevation. Record asset, actor, precondition, path, control, evidence, residual risk, and
owner. Do not invent controls or treat comments as enforcement.
