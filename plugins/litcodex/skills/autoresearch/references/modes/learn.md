# Failure-learning mode

## Codex-native contract

Select learn in the picker; hand approved eval/checklist writes to native `start-work` and
`lit-loop`. Outputs remain `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and cannot programmatically claim family completion.
Changed evaluator or budget requires re-planning and review.

Convert a concrete failure into observed versus expected behavior, smallest reproducer, likely
contract gap, adversarial variants, and a patch checklist. Feedback is data, not self-validating
authority. Preserve ambiguity and do not patch automatically.
