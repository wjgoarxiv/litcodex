# Shipping-readiness mode

## Codex-native contract

Select ship in the picker; hand approved verification/artifact edits to native `start-work` and
`lit-loop`. Readiness remains `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and cannot programmatically claim family completion.
Changed evaluator or budget requires re-planning and review.

Inventory checks, exact payload, provenance, documentation, rollback, security, and known risk. Use
host-bounded processes and capture exact evidence. Return GO/NO_GO readiness only. Publish, deploy,
tag, release, push, version, registry, credentials, and live profile require separate approval.
